BEAD CLEANUP - QUICK PROTOCOL (kit insert, rev C)

1. Vortex beads for 30 s before use. Bring to room temperature.
2. Add 0.045 mL beads to each 0.025 mL sample (24 samples, plate columns 1-3). Pipette-mix 10 times.
3. Incubate 5 minutes at room temperature.
4. Place plate on magnet for 2 minutes. Remove and discard supernatant (approx. 0.065 mL).
5. Add 0.18 mL of 80% ethanol. Wait 30 seconds. Remove and discard. Repeat once.
6. Air-dry 3 minutes. Do not over-dry.
7. Remove from magnet. Add 0.022 mL elution buffer, mix 10 times, incubate 2 minutes.
8. Return to magnet 1 minute. Transfer 0.02 mL eluate to a new plate.
Store at -20 C.
