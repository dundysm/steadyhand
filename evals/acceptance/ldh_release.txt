LDH release assay on culture supernatants.

Culture plate: 96 wells, 120 uL medium over the cells in each.
Transfer 50 uL of supernatant from every well to the same well of a fresh flat-bottom plate. Do not disturb the cells: stay near the top of the liquid.
Add 50 uL of assay reagent to each well of the new plate. Cover from light, 30 minutes at room temperature.
Add 50 uL stop solution to each well. Read 490 nm within the hour.
