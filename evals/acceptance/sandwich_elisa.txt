Sandwich ELISA from antibody pair, one 96-well plate, all wells.

Coat: 100 uL capture antibody per well, seal, overnight.
Wash: empty the wells, fill with 400 uL wash buffer, empty again; three times in total.
Block: 300 uL reagent diluent per well, at least 1 hour. Wash as above.
Samples and standards: 100 uL per well from the matching wells of a dilution plate (150 uL in each). 2 hours. Wash.
Detection antibody: 100 uL per well, 2 hours. Wash.
Streptavidin-HRP: 100 uL per well, 20 minutes, out of the light. Wash.
Substrate: 100 uL per well, 20 minutes.
Stop: 50 uL per well. Tap to mix and read at 450 nm.
