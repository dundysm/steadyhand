660 nm protein assay, microplate.

10 uL of each standard and sample into the assay plate: 8 standards in column 1 and 40 samples in columns 2-6, taken from the same positions of a stock plate (50 uL per well).
150 uL assay reagent to each well. Cover, shake one minute at medium speed, 5 minutes at room temperature, read at 660 nm.
