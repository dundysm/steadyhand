Detergent-compatible Lowry-type assay in a microplate.

5 uL of standards and samples into a clean dry plate (48 wells, columns 1-6; they come from columns 1-6 of a stock plate with 30 uL per well).
25 uL reagent A' into each well.
200 uL reagent B into each well. Mix.
After 15 minutes read at 750 nm.
Reagent A' and reagent B are in two troughs.
