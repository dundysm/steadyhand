Bead cleanup of 48 PCR reactions (50 uL each, columns 1-6 of the PCR plate), 1.8x beads.

1. Add 90 uL beads to each reaction, pipette-mix 10 times, leave 5 minutes.
2. On the magnet until clear, then take off and discard the liquid.
3. Still on the magnet: add 200 uL of 70% ethanol, wait 30 seconds, remove and discard. Do the ethanol wash twice in total.
4. Let the beads dry a few minutes.
5. Off the magnet: add 40 uL elution buffer, mix 10 times, back on the magnet.
6. Move 38 uL of the clear eluate to a fresh plate.
Beads, ethanol and elution buffer are each in their own trough.
