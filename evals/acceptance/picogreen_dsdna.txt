Fluorescent dsDNA quantitation in a black 96-well plate, 200 uL final volume.

32 DNA samples in columns 1-4 of a sample plate, 20 uL each.
- 95 uL TE into columns 1-4 of the assay plate.
- 5 uL of each DNA into the matching well (so the DNA is in 100 uL TE).
- 100 uL of dye working solution (dye diluted 200-fold in TE, in a trough) into each of those wells. Mix.
- 5 minutes at room temperature out of the light, then read.
