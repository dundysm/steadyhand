Library quantification by qPCR, 20 uL reactions.

Per well: 12 uL master mix with primers already added, 4 uL water, 4 uL template.
Templates: six DNA standards in A1-F1 of a template plate and 16 diluted libraries in columns 2-3, 20 uL each. Everything is run in duplicate.
I combine master mix and water first (16 uL per reaction, enough for 50 reactions, 800 uL in one deep well) and put 16 uL in each well of the qPCR plate, then add 4 uL of template. Duplicates sit in neighbouring columns: template column 1 goes to qPCR columns 1-2, column 2 to 3-4, column 3 to 5-6 (only rows A-F for the standards).
