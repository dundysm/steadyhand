BCA protein assay, plate format, standard 37 C protocol.

24 lysates sit in columns 1-3 of a sample plate (about 60 uL each). Run each in duplicate.
- 25 uL of each sample into a clear flat-bottom assay plate, duplicates side by side (sample columns 1-3 go to assay columns 1-2, 3-4, 5-6).
- 200 uL working reagent (A and B already mixed 50:1, in a reservoir) into every well that got sample.
- Shake 30 seconds, cover, 37 C for 30 minutes, cool, read at 562 nm.
